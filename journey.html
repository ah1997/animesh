Life is a Journey

Physics has taken me to a fair number of places — and the road in between has often been the best part of the trip.

Here are some memorable journeys from academic and non-academic visits: mountains in the north-east, a long bike ride down south, and a summer in Italy. Follow the dotted line.

3Countries
6Indian states
2023

North Sikkim

Sikkim, India

The road climbs past Lachung until the air thins and the valley opens out. Cold, bright, and quiet in a way the plains never are — the kind of place that resets your sense of scale.

22 – 26 March 2024

Rishab & Darjeeling

West Bengal, India

Pine forests, tea slopes and a morning when the clouds finally cleared off Kanchenjunga. Five days of walking, cheap momos and very good sunrises.

7 – 10 September 2024

Bike trip: Bengaluru → Coorg

Karnataka, India

Out of the city before sunrise and into coffee country by afternoon. Wet roads, mist hanging in the plantations, and the particular satisfaction of arriving somewhere entirely under your own throttle.

15 September 2024

Dhondenling

Karnataka, India

A day trip to the Tibetan settlement — prayer flags over maize fields, butter tea, and a monastery that feels a very long way from the rest of Karnataka.

23 – 28 February 2025

Dibrugarh, Guwahati & Pelling

Assam & Sikkim, India

Tea gardens along the Brahmaputra, then west and up: six days that ended with Kanchenjunga filling the whole window at Pelling. A long way to travel in one week, and worth every hour of it.

9 – 12 May 2025

Rolep

East Sikkim, India

A small village by the river, almost no phone signal, and the sound of water all night. The best kind of place to go when you have been staring at a screen for too many months.

12 – 19 July 2025

Italy — Rome, Florence, Pisa

Italy · STATPHYS29, Florence

My first trip outside India — a poster at STATPHYS29 in Florence, and the rest of the week spent walking. Rome in the heat, the Duomo at dusk, and a slightly unreasonable detour to Pisa.

1 – 2 November 2025

Parasnath

Jharkhand, India

An overnight climb up Shikharji to reach the summit temples by first light — nine hours of stone steps and a view over Jharkhand that made the legs forgive everything.

14 – 19 March 2026

Meghalaya

Meghalaya, India

Living root bridges, limestone caves and a great deal of rain — the abode of clouds earning its name. Six days, endless steps down into the gorges, and water clear enough to see every stone.
