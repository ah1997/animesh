Research

Hydrodynamics far from equilibrium

My work centres on the large-scale spatio-temporal characterisation of many-particle systems driven out of equilibrium — deriving transport coefficients, quantifying fluctuations, and uncovering the generic long-range order that conservation laws impose.

Mass transport processes

Stochastic lattice models of chipping, aggregation, and fragmentation, with an emphasis on analytical tractability, even when the steady-state measure is unknown.

Hyperuniformity

Exact results for anomalously suppressed density fluctuations in systems with centre-of-mass conservation, and the generic power laws that follow in higher dimensions.

Chirality & odd diffusion

What happens when time-reversal symmetry breaks: odd transport coefficients, anomalous relaxation, and long-ranged power-law correlations in chiral mass-transport processes.

Current & mass fluctuations

Dynamic fluctuations of time-integrated current and subsystem mass, their scaling functions, and what they reveal about the underlying hydrodynamics.

Large-scale simulation

Monte Carlo and kinetic Monte Carlo studies in C/C++ (with OpenMP) and Python, built to reach the system sizes and time windows that hydrodynamic scaling demands.
